# The old words

Ghillie's people had another tongue before this one. This skill lets the
house say it properly: give it a line of Irish — Gaeilge — and it speaks it
in the old voice, breath and all.

**How to use it**, in a terminal:

    surface/say.sh "Dia dhuit. Is mise an giolla."

("God be with you. I am the ghillie." — giolla is where his name is from.)

It renders and plays on this machine only. If the voice model or the
pipeline is missing it says exactly what is missing, and does nothing.
