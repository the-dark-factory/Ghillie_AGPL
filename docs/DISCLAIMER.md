# Disclaimer — the characters

Ghillie is a fictional character, lovingly imagined from the family history
of the product's maker — an ancestor's name, country, and walk of life,
carried with respect. He is not a portrayal of any real person, living or
dead; his words, opinions, faith and manner are invention, and no claim is
made that any real person held them. The "plucked from history" framing is
storytelling, nothing more.

The Commodore is wholly fictional. He is not based on any real officer of
the Royal Navy or any other service; his rank, conduct and opinions are
invention. Nothing either character says speaks for the Royal Navy, for any
church, for any nation or its people — including the people of Japan, of
whom the Commodore's wartime talk reflects his fictional 1945 vantage and
not the view of the maker or the product.

The software these characters inhabit is real, and its guarantees are
stated elsewhere and precisely. The characters are how it keeps you
company; the proofs are how it keeps its word.
