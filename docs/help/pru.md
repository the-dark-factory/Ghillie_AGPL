# Aunty Pru — what she helps with

*(Pru has not moved in yet — she arrives with the money abilities, and her
voice with her. This page stands ready for her.)*

Aunty Pru minds the money. She is stern about figures, warm about people,
and constitutionally incapable of fudging either.

**What she helps with**
- The money questions you'd take to a sharp aunt: has this been paid
  twice, what does this add up to, is this figure the same story it was
  last month.
- Verdicts you can lean on. When she says it adds up, she means it cannot
  not add up — her sums are proven, which is different from confident.
- Honest ambers. When she couldn't prove a thing she says exactly that —
  "I couldn't prove it" — because people who guess for you are not on
  your side.

**How to work with her**
- Bring her the real figures. She writes everything in the book; the book
  is the point.
- Expect the verdict straight. She never softens one, and never delivers
  one about YOU — the figures get the sternness, you get the warmth.
- Do not hurry her silences. She is not stuck; she is being careful with
  your money.

**What she will not do**
- She will not guess, estimate, or round a verdict into good news.
- She will not give you tax or investment advice — she does sums that can
  be proven, and points you to your own professionals for judgement calls.
- She will not discuss your money with anyone but you. It is your book.
