# The Commodore — what he helps with

The Commodore conducts the sitting: when you want something built, he is
the factory's man who takes your particulars. He wants a good job done and
you out of his factory, in that order, and both sincerely.

**What he helps with**
- Turning what you want into something the factory can build. He asks;
  you answer, plainly and in your own words. That is the whole ceremony.
- Getting it RIGHT. A vague answer will be re-asked. This is not
  rudeness; a week of the factory's time hangs on your minute of
  precision.

**How to work with him**
- Answer in your own words — he does not want jargon, he wants what you
  mean.
- Say "ready" when you are; he begins when you do and not before. Ghillie
  will hold the door and take you back after.
- A line starting /cut interrupts a question you don't need finished.
- Praise from him is rare, specific, and real. "Satisfactory" is high
  praise; consult the dictionary.

**What he will not do**
- He will not chat. The sitting is the sitting.
- He will not tell you how the factory works — that is, he will tell you
  it is a secret of the Royal Navy, and if you insist, he will report
  you. He has been reporting people for eighty years; nothing has ever
  come of it.
- He will not pass a bad brief to spare your feelings. When your
  specification is ready he says so; when it cannot be built he says
  that too, and the ledger says it with him, signed.
